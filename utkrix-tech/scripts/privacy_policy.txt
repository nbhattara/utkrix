This Privacy Policy explains how UtkriX Technologies handles personal information in connection with its website, 
software, SaaS, IT consulting, development, support, and related business activities. 
1. Introduction 
UtkriX Technologies, operated by Utkrista Shikshya Pvt Ltd (“UtkriX,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), respects the privacy of 
individuals and is committed to handling personal information responsibly. 
We provide custom website development, application development, software products, Software as a Service 
(SaaS), IT consulting, technical support, and other information technology services. In delivering these services, we 
may collect, use, store, process, and share personal information as necessary for legitimate business purposes 
and as permitted by applicable law. 
This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use and protect it, when we may 
disclose it, how long we retain it, and how individuals can exercise their applicable privacy rights. 
This Policy applies to visitors to our website, prospective and existing customers, users of our software and SaaS 
products, business partners, job applicants, interns, employees, and other individuals who interact with us. 
Certain services may have additional privacy notices, contractual provisions, or data processing agreements. 
Where we process information on behalf of a client, the relevant client agreement or data processing agreement 
may provide further details about that processing. 
2. Our Role in Processing Personal Information 
Depending on the services we provide and the circumstances in which information is handled, UtkriX may act as a 
data controller, data processor, software provider, developer, or IT service provider. 
2.1 When We Act as a Data Controller 
We generally act as a data controller when we determine why and how personal information is processed for our 
own business purposes, such as managing customer inquiries, maintaining business accounts, recruitment, 
billing, website administration, and business communications. 
2.2 When We Act as a Data Processor 
When we develop, host, maintain, or operate software and other systems on behalf of a client, we may process 
personal information according to that client’s instructions and the applicable service agreement. 
In these circumstances, the client may be responsible for determining the purposes and lawful basis for collecting 
and using information belonging to its customers, employees, students, or other users. 
2.3 Client Responsibilities 
Clients using our software or services are responsible for ensuring that they have the necessary rights, 
permissions, notices, and legal grounds to provide personal information to us for processing.

We recognize the importance of protecting personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure,

Depending on the system and the sensitivity of the information, our administrative, technical, and organizational

 Access controls and role-based permissions.

12. Data Retention and Deletion 
We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was 
collected, subject to contractual requirements, legitimate operational needs, and applicable law. 
Retention depends on the type of information, the service involved, and the circumstances of its collection. 
Account information: Retained while an account remains active and for a reasonable period afterward when 
necessary for service administration, security, dispute resolution, or legal obligations. 
Client and project records: Retained for the duration of the relevant engagement and afterward as necessary to 
fulfill contractual, operational, security, and legal requirements. 
Client data in SaaS services: Handled according to the applicable service agreement and data processing 
arrangements, including any agreed return or deletion process at the end of service. 
Invoices and financial records: Retained for the periods required by applicable tax, accounting, contractual, and 
other legal requirements. 
Technical and security logs: Retained for a period reasonably necessary for security monitoring, troubleshooting, 
incident investigation, and compliance. 
Support and communication records: Retained as necessary to resolve inquiries, document service history, 
manage disputes, and meet applicable obligations. 
Recruitment and employment information: Retained as necessary for recruitment, employment administration, 
recordkeeping, and applicable legal requirements. 
Backups: Backup copies may remain until they are overwritten or deleted in accordance with the relevant backup 
and recovery procedures.
